The Sussex County Fairgrounds, on Plains Road in Augusta, spread over more than 150 acres and hold over 25 permanent buildings. A nonprofit, the Sussex County Farm and Horse Show Association, runs the grounds and plans the fair held there; its executive board and directors are all volunteers, and the association says the grounds get no money from government. The fair goes by two names at once, the New Jersey State Fair and the Sussex County Farm and Horse Show, and the state Department of Agriculture lists it among New Jersey's agricultural fairs as the "Sussex County Fair." [@fair-mission] [@fair-history] [@njda-fairs]

It started with horses. The association's timeline records a three-day horse show put on by Branchville businessmen in September 1919, and a Sussex County Horse Show that began at Ross's Corner in Augusta in 1924 and moved to a private track in Branchville in 1926. The horse show came first and was later joined with farm exhibits, and the Sussex County Farm & Horse Show was incorporated as a nonprofit on April 26, 1940. No fair was held from 1942 to 1945 because of World War II. [@fair-history]

In 1963 the directors bought 126 acres in Augusta for a new home. The grounds were dedicated in August 1976, when the Richards Exhibit Building was the only permanent building on them. The fair took on the extra name "New Jersey State Fair" in 1999, and that name became a registered trademark. The 2020 fair was canceled because of COVID-19, and in 2021 the fairgrounds served as a COVID-19 vaccination site. [@fair-history]

## Why it matters

According to the fair's agriculture history page, archived in April 2026, farming in Sussex County goes back to the earliest settlers. The page says dairy farms were the most common kind in the 1800s and early 1900s, especially after the railroads reached the county, and that even in the 1950s residents "could boast that there were more cows than people." Over the sixty years before that page was written, it says, dairy farms have dwindled while farms raising hay, horses, small animals, Christmas trees and ornamental shrubs have grown in number, and it also points to wineries, orchards, pick-your-own farms and farmers' markets that draw visitors. [@fair-ag-history]

The USDA's 2022 Census of Agriculture counted 1,052 farms in Sussex County, averaging 68 acres, and found that 95 percent were family farms. Hay and haylage took up more acreage than any other crop in the profile, and the county ranked second in the state in sales of milk from cows, among counties whose rankings the USDA could publish. [@usda-2022]

The association's mission puts showcasing "New Jersey's agriculture" first and also includes promoting youth development. During the fair, 4-H clubs use the Shotwell Building for projects and activities, and the Snook Agricultural Museum, run by the Sussex County Agricultural Society since 1983, shows antique farm tools "from the turn of the 20th century." The association's livestock count for 2019 listed 299 dairy cattle, 273 goats, 267 sheep and 545 horses, among other animals. The Sussex County Board of Agriculture holds its monthly meetings, except in May, August and December, in the fairgrounds' Administration Building. [@fair-mission] [@fair-snook] [@fair-history] [@scba]

## Worth a visit?

Yes. As archived in spring 2026, the association's visitor pages described barns where livestock were on display before and after their shows; fairgoers could get close to the pens but were asked to keep their hands out of them, and to ask the owners and handlers in the barns which animals were "approachable." They also described a carnival, a farmers' market and the Snook museum, which opens daily during the fair. Pets are not allowed, though service animals are. [@fair-visitor] [@fair-snook]

The grounds host events outside the fair too: the 2026 schedule listed a series of horse shows between April and October, and the home page, as archived in June 2026, said "Something is always happening" there. Check the official site for current dates, tickets and events. [@fair-horse-shows] [@fair-home]
