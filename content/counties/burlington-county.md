Burlington County is the largest county in New Jersey by land area, running from the Delaware River to the Atlantic coast at the Great Bay. Its county seat is Mount Holly, and it is made up of 40 municipalities: 3 cities, 6 boroughs and 31 townships. [@bc-profile] [@census-gazetteer-counties] [@census-gazetteer-cousubs]

English settlers arriving on the ship Kent founded the town of Burlington in 1677, which became the capital of the province of West Jersey, and the county was formally incorporated in 1694. The Lenni-Lenape are the county's original people. [@bc-profile] [@bc-history]

Burlington is one of seven counties that share the Pinelands National Reserve, and its pine woods, streams and lakes draw visitors. The county is known for farming, from cranberries to Whitesbog Village, which the county calls the home of the blueberry. [@bc-profile] [@bc-history] [@pc-size]

## Why it matters

Burlington County's history is on view across the county: buildings from colonial times onward, the Historic Prison Museum in Mount Holly, a National Historic Landmark designed by architect Robert Mills, and Timbuctoo, a free Black community that dates to before the Civil War. [@bc-history]
